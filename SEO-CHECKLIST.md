## 📞 Emergency Contacts

### If You Notice:
- **Sudden traffic drop**: Check Search Console for penalties
- **Ranking loss**: Review competitor changes, check backlinks
- **Site not appearing**: Verify in Search Console, check robots.txt
- **Slow loading**: Run PageSpeed Insights, optimize images

### Support Resources:
- Google Search Central: https://developers.google.com/search
- Bing Webmaster Help: https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help
- Schema.org Documentation: https://schema.org

## 📝 Notes

- SEO is a long-term strategy - expect 3-6 months for significant results
- Quality content and legitimate backlinks are most important
- Never buy backlinks or use black-hat techniques
- Mobile-first indexing is crucial - always test on mobile
- Page speed affects rankings - optimize regularly
- User experience (UX) impacts SEO - prioritize usability
