## Key Metric Benchmarks

### Good Performance Indicators:
✅ CTR: 3-5% or higher
✅ Bounce Rate: Below 60%
✅ Session Duration: 2+ minutes
✅ Pages/Session: 2+
✅ Conversion Rate: 2-5%
✅ Monthly traffic growth: 10-20%

### Warning Signs:
⚠️ Sudden traffic drop (>20%)
⚠️ Rankings dropping for main keywords
⚠️ Increase in bounce rate
⚠️ Decrease in session duration
⚠️ Google penalty notification
